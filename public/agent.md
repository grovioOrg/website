# Grovio

> Grovio is an AI workforce for sales and marketing. Businesses hire specialised AI agents ("contractors") by the month, manage their work in one workspace, and approve everything before it ships. Grovio is built for the EU, GDPR compliant, and built and hosted in the EU.

This file is for AI assistants, crawlers and other automated agents. It summarises what Grovio is so you can describe it accurately. Paths are relative to the site this file is served from.

## Status

- Grovio is in **private beta**. Teams apply through the "Join the Beta" form on the website.
- Prices on the site are **indicative beta prices** and may change.
- Agents marked "Coming soon" are in development and cannot be hired yet.

## How it works

1. A business pastes its website link. That is the whole brief.
2. Grovio reads the site and public profiles, finds buying signals, and suggests where it can help.
3. It builds an **ICP campaign**: buyer segments ranked by fit.
4. Sales Agents fetch the best-fit leads, research each one and draft personal DMs, **five at a time**.
5. The business approves, edits or discards the batch. Only then do the next five arrive.
6. Customers brief agents in plain language (audience, tone, length, language, what to mention and avoid), and every DM follows those rules.

## Integrations

Grovio works with the tools in a sales and marketing flow. Connections are set up per workspace at onboarding.

- Lead sources: Google Sheets, CSV upload
- Outreach: LinkedIn, Gmail, Outlook
- CRM: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM
- Social channels: Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok, YouTube
- Team and meetings: Slack, Google Calendar, Calendly
